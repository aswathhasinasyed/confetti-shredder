Common questions

Does it delete my real photos or files?
Not unless you turn on Real Shred (the REAL switch, off by default). Normally only an in-app copy is shredded. With Real Shred on, the original is deleted after the shred, and iOS or the app asks you to confirm every time. Deleted photos stay in Photos → Recently Deleted for 30 days.

I don't hear anything.
Check the speaker button in the top corner, and your phone's silent switch: Confetti Shredder stays quiet when your phone is on silent.

I don't feel the vibration.
Haptics need an iPhone with a Taptic Engine and System Haptics turned on in Settings → Sounds & Haptics.

My tray is empty.
Tap "Reset demo" to bring the sample files back, or "Select file or photo" to add your own.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Confetti Shredder does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app makes no network connections.
  • Photos and files you choose are opened with Apple's system pickers. The app reads a copy only to draw the preview on screen. It keeps that copy in memory while the app is open and never uploads it or saves it elsewhere.
  • Real Shred (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, the app asks for Full Access to your photo library so it can delete the photos you shred. It deletes only the photo or file you just shredded, and only after you confirm: iOS asks before a photo is deleted, and the app asks before a file is deleted. Deleted photos stay in Photos → Recently Deleted for 30 days. Real Shred never reads, uploads or deletes anything else, and nothing leaves your device.
  • Recording (optional). The record button uses Apple's screen recording to capture this app's own screen and sound, never the microphone or other apps. The video stays on your device; it is only saved to Photos or sent somewhere if you choose that in the share sheet.
  • Your control. You can turn Real Shred off at any time with the REAL switch, and remove photo access in Settings → Apps → Shredder → Photos. The app keeps only its own settings (such as whether Real Shred is on) on your device.
  • Nothing leaves your device. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request, export or delete.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
